# 🏷️ Attribution & Usage Guidelines

We welcome developers, students, and creators to experiment with, fork, and learn from **Spidey Testimonials**!

## 📜 Dual Licensing Model
- **Code Engine (MIT License)**: The underlying WebGL 3D math, analytical IK solver, cloth physics, and Web Component architecture are free to use and adapt under the MIT License.
- **3D Assets & Visual Design (CC BY-NC 4.0)**: The 3D assets (spiderman.glb, Mic.glb), textures, shaders, and visual design system are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International**.

## 📌 Requirements for Forks & Displays
If you showcase, fork, or adapt this project:
1. **Credit Original Work**: Retain credit to **Starkitekt** and link back to [https://github.com/starkitekt/spidey-testimony](https://github.com/starkitekt/spidey-testimony).
2. **Non-Commercial Use**: You may not sell the 3D models or visual design assets as a paid template or commercial product without prior written permission.
